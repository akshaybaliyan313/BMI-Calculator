📘 How to Calculate BMI

BMI = Weight (kg) / (Height (m) × Height (m))

Example: 65 kg and 170 cm (1.70 m) → 65 / (1.70 × 1.70) = 22.5

⚠️ BMI is a general screening measure for adults and is not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a doctor for health advice.
